Why it looks like this

Borrowed fromThe patternWhere it is in LeewayWhy it works
Monzo budgetsThe amount left is the biggest thing on the row, not the amount spentBig "2 left" number on every courseRemaining framing is what you act on. People protect a labelled pot (mental accounting).
Duolingo heartsRed hearts that go grey when lostSix hearts per course: red (left), red outline (planned), grey (used)A picture is read before a number, and a grey heart stings more than "81%" (loss aversion). Streaks, gems and the guilt owl are left out.
FlightyOne status word, always from the same short listSafe, Burning fast, Last life, Out of lives, Over the limit, in its colour, no badgeA fixed vocabulary is learned once and then scanned, not read.
YNABGreen, amber and red mean state and nothing elseOnly the number and the status word carry state colour. Buttons and text are ink.When colour only ever means risk, risk is seen at once (isolation effect).
CitymapperThe verdict first, the detail secondThe line under the field: "This uses 1 life. 4 left, 8 weeks to go."Feedback at the moment of choice changes the choice; after the save it is only a record.
Things 3 and Apple RemindersOne entry field, hairline lists, no shadows, system fontField and "Log a skip" at the top, courses as one hairline list, the phone's own font (SF Pro on iPhone)Fewer choices make faster decisions (Hick's law). The system font makes it feel native, not like a website.
Gmail Undo sendUndo instead of "Are you sure?""Skip logged. Undo" pinned at the bottom for 5 secondsA wrong tap costs nothing, so logging stays quick and honest.

Left out on purpose: Apple Fitness rings (a ring is a goal you close, but lives only go down, and two shapes would compete with the hearts) and Strava or BeReal recaps (they suit the Release 2 weekly check).

ElementWord budgetWhy
Landing, above the button30 words or fewerA skimmer reads about 4 to 5 words a second, so 5 seconds covers the headline, one line and the button.
Course row10 words or fewerName, number, one status word, weeks to go. More than that and rows stop being scannable.
Skip preview15 words or fewerIt is read while the thumb is over the button.
Risk line8 words or fewerOne course, one state.
